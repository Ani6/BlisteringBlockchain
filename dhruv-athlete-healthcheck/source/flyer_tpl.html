Athlete and Physique Health Check-Up

Heart, muscle, hormone, long-term health and tendon assessment for athletes, gym members and bodybuilders.

NABL accredited since 2011

Heart

Treadmill stress test (TMT), resting ECG, NT-proBNP, hs-troponin

Fitness in METs, blood-pressure response and a cardiac baseline.

Cholesterol and long-term health

Apo B, Lp(a), lipid profile, hs-CRP, HbA1c, fasting insulin, homocysteine, uric acid, cystatin C

Apo B counts the particles that build plaque. Lp(a) is inherited and tested once.

Tendons and ligaments

Ultrasound by a trained MSK radiologist: shoulder, elbow, hip, knee, ankle

Rotator cuff, patellar and Achilles tendons, ligaments, plantar fascia. No radiation.

Muscle and recovery

CK (CPK), CK-MB, myoglobin, LDH, AST, ALT, potassium

Read against athlete ranges: the CK upper limit in athletes is about twice that of non-athletes.

Hormones and blood

Testosterone, LH, FSH, SHBG, oestradiol, prolactin, cortisol, IGF-1, thyroid, haemogram, ferritin, vitamin D, B12

Haematocrit, liver and hormone checks for strength athletes, in confidence.

Urine drug screen (optional)

Stimulants, cannabinoids, opiates, tramadol and more, each shown with its WADA status

A clinical screen, not a doping test. Positives need confirmation.
