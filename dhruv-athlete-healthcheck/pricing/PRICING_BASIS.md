## How the prices were set

1. Researched current walk-in and online prices for every test in each plan from Nagpur pages of national chains (Dr Lal PathLabs, Metropolis, Max Lab, Agilus, Redcliffe, Tata 1mg, Apollo, Thyrocare, Healthians) and Nagpur labs and hospitals.
2. Re-checked the 12 most expensive items independently with fresh searches; 8 were confirmed and 4 adjusted (IGF-1, myoglobin, vitamin D, urine drug panel).
3. Benchmarked 36 packages from 13 providers, 18 of them Nagpur listings. Blood-only packages sell at 50-80% below the sum of their tests; packages with TMT, ultrasound or consultations sell at 25-45% below.
4. Applied about 70% off the blood-test sum and 35% off the procedure sum (ECG, TMT, ultrasound, consultation), then set prices at or just under the result and within Nagpur's market bands: basic gym panels Rs 900-2,300, advanced cardiac or hormone panels Rs 2,600-4,500 (blood only), premium hospital and executive packages Rs 5,500-13,000.
